Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SOHEL VISUALS website handles information.

1. Information you provide

When you use our inquiry form, your browser prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. This website does not store the submitted information in a website database.

2. WhatsApp and email

If you contact us through WhatsApp or email, we may use the information you voluntarily provide to respond to your inquiry, discuss your project and deliver agreed services.

WhatsApp and other external providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or a first-party analytics system. External services such as Google Fonts, Google Drive and WhatsApp may receive technical information when you access them.

4. Information security

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, PINs, OTPs or sensitive identity information through our inquiry form.

5. Data retention

Messages and project materials shared directly with us may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, complete projects, maintain business records or resolve disputes.

6. Children's privacy

If you are not legally able to enter into a service agreement in your location, a parent or legal guardian should handle contractual and payment arrangements where required by law.

7. Policy updates

This policy may change when our website or data practices change. The updated date will be revised when changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: sohelvisuals.business@gmail.com
